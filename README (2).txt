Build Customer Support Automations — Submission Package
=========================================================

Contents:
1. Report.docx / Report.pdf
   Full project report covering Parts A–M: selected scenario, customer
   queries, workflow, automated responses, automation description,
   communication/UX practices, AI-assisted improvements, testing
   results, review checklist, scenario analysis, and reflection.

2. website/index.html
   The working Order Status chatbot interface (open this file in any
   browser). Includes a welcome message, FAQ quick-buttons, an
   automated response area, and a "Talk to a person" escalation option.

How to submit:
- Fill in your Name, Register Number, and Department & Year on the
  cover of Report.docx before submitting (these were left blank as
  they were not provided).
- Upload this whole folder (as this ZIP) to Google Drive or your
  chosen platform, or submit the ZIP directly if the form allows file
  upload.
- Take a screenshot or two of website/index.html open in your browser
  (showing a sample chat) and add them into the Report before final
  submission, per the "Screenshots" requirement.
- Submit the link/file through the Google Form as instructed in the
  task sheet.
